   Your Way (bypass.440.info):
   Xbox → bypass.440.info DoH → Returns YOUR VPS IP

💡 WHY THIS WORKS:

   xbox-dns.ru blocks ISP by:
   1. DNS returns their IP (not Xbox IP)
   2. Their proxy forwards to real Xbox
   
   Your setup does THE SAME:
   1. DNS returns YOUR VPS IP (not Xbox IP)
   2. YOUR HAProxy forwards to real Xbox
   
   Result: ISP can't block what they can't see! 🎉
